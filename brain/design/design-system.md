# Design System

## Colors

### Brand Colors
- **Primary:** #0C3C49 (Deep teal) - Buttons, headers, primary actions
- **Accent:** #2D8C3C (Green) - Success states, CTAs
- **Cream:** #F9F6F2 (Soft background)
- **Text:** #182022 (Charcoal) - Primary text
- **Neutral:** #F5F5F5 - Cards, borders

### Semantic Colors
- **Red Flag:** #E74C3C (Dangerous men posts)
- **Green Flag:** #2D8C3C (Safe)
- **Neutral Flag:** #95A5A6 (Neutral/Unknown)

## Typography
- **Font Family:** Nunito / Inter (via Google Fonts)
- **Headlines:** Bold, 24-32px
- **Body:** Regular, 16-18px, high legibility
- **Captions:** Medium, 14px
- **Small:** Regular, 12px

## Corners & Shadows
- **Border Radius:** 16-20px (rounded cards)
- **Shadows:** Soft ambient shadows for depth
- **Elevation:** Material Design principles

## UI Components
- Buttons: Large rounded, filled primary (deep teal) + ghost secondary
- Inputs: Rounded, large touch targets (min 44×44), left icons, labels + helper text
- Cards: Material cards with 16-20px radius, soft shadows
- Bottom Nav: 5 tabs (Home, Feed, Add, Forum, Events)
- Top App Bar: Left profile avatar, right Chat + Bell icons
